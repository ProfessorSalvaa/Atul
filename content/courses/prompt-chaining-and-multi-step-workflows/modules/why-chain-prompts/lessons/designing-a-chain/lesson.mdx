This lesson is part of **Why Chain Prompts At All** in _Prompt Chaining and Multi-Step Workflows_. By the end, you should be able to put the following into practice:

- A common effective pattern: one step extracts/structures raw information, a second step drafts from that structured input, a third step critiques the draft against explicit criteria
- Pass structured data (JSON) between steps rather than freeform prose whenever a later step needs to reliably parse the earlier output
- Keep the critique step's criteria explicit and specific — a vague 'is this good?' critique step rarely catches anything a first pass didn't already catch

```text
Step 1 (extract): 'Extract every claim and its source from this article as JSON: [{claim, source}]'
Step 2 (draft): 'Using this JSON of claims, draft a 200-word summary citing each source inline.'
Step 3 (critique): 'Check the draft against the original claims JSON — flag any claim that is missing, altered, or unattributed.'
```
