This lesson is part of **Building Reliable Automations** in _AI Agents and Automation Fundamentals_. By the end, you should be able to put the following into practice:

- Every automated step needs an explicit definition of 'done correctly' that can be checked programmatically, not just 'the model said it worked'
- Build in a timeout and a maximum-retry count for every step — an automation with no limits can silently hang or loop indefinitely
- On failure, fail loudly (alert, log, halt) rather than silently continuing with a partial or wrong result

```text
Step: "Categorize and route incoming support ticket"
Success condition: category is one of the 5 valid enum values AND confidence score >= 0.7
Failure condition: anything else
On failure: route to human queue with a flag, do not auto-close the ticket
```
